Adobo Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 500g of pork
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup of oyster sauce
  • 1/4 teaspoon of sugar
  • 1/8 head of garlic, minced
  • 1 medium onion, minced
  • 1-3 pieces of bay leaves
  • salt
  • pepper
  • oil
  • 1 cup of water
  • 1-3 pieces of potatoes

Instructions:

  1. in a pot saute the onion and garlic for a minute
  2. add the pork and cook until light brown
  3. add the soy sauce and oyster, let it simmer for a minute or two before adding the water
  4. add the bay leaves, salt, and pepper to taste and let it simmer for about 30-45 mins on low heat setting
  5. before adding the potatoes remove the bay leaves first, after adding the potatoes let it cook until tender while adjusting the taste with the use of sugar
  6. once the pork is tender and the sauce has thickened, turn off the heat and serve
